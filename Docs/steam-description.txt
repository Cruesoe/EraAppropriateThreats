[b]Keeps raids and other threats in step with your colony's technology, so a tribal village isn't wiped out by space pirates.[/b]

Era Appropriate Threats is a spiritual successor to [b]Ignorance Is Bliss[/b]. It's a new mod written from scratch, with the same idea at its heart and fixes for what players have asked for over the years.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Follows your progress:[/b] Your colony's tech level rises with your research and never drops below the level you started at.
[*][b]Raids in range:[/b] A raid that would come from a faction outside your range is given to a faction inside it, so you still face your share of raids.
[*][b]Your choice per faction:[/b] Every faction, including those added by other mods, can be set to come by tech level, always or never.
[*][b]Mech clusters and infestations:[/b] Mech clusters, crashed ship parts and infestations follow the rules you set for mechanoids and insects.
[*][b]Quest sites:[/b] Hostile quest sites, including siege camps from VFE - Medieval 2, only use factions in your range.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Tech level:[/b] Choose the share of each level's research you must finish (50% by default), your highest research, your faction's own level, or a fixed range.
[*][b]Range:[/b] Choose how many levels above and below yours can come. By default that's one above and any number below.
[*][b]No faction in range:[/b] The closest faction comes by default, or you can skip the raid instead.
[*][b]Occasional surprises:[/b] Give each raid a chance to ignore the limit. This is 0% by default.
[*][b]Quest raiders:[/b] Replace quest raiders from outside your range. This is off by default, and the quest text still names the original faction.
[*][b]Traders and visitors:[/b] Limit trade caravans and visitors in the same way. This is off by default.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Not compatible with Ignorance Is Bliss because they control the same system, so use one or the other.
Events from other mods that pick their own faction aren't limited, and Rim War and Winston Waves aren't supported.
Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
